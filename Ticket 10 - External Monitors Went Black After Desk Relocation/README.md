Help Desk Technician – Lab 08
External Monitors Went Black After Desk Relocation
Issue Description
The user reported that both external monitors remained black after moving their desk. The laptop
display continued working normally, but both external displays failed when connected through the
docking station.
Business Impact
The user was forced to work on a single laptop screen, reducing productivity.
Troubleshooting Performed
Confirmed the laptop display was working. Confirmed the issue began immediately after the desk
was moved. Determined a physical connection was the most likely cause. Guided the user to
disconnect and reconnect the monitor and docking station cables.
Resolution
After reseating all display and docking station cables, both external monitors were restored. The
issue was caused by a loose connection after the desk relocation.
