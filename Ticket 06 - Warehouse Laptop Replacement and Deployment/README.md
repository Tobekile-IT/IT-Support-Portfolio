Shared Warehouse Laptop Replacement Incident
Report
Reported by: Mike Reeves
Department: Facilities
Location: Floor 2
Incident Summary
The shared warehouse inventory laptop failed to power on. The device was essential for stock
scanning and inventory logging, blocking warehouse operations.
Business Impact
Warehouse inventory activities were halted, multiple employees could not continue stock scanning,
and inventory deadlines were at risk.
Investigation
Confirmed no power, no lights, and no fan activity. Verified a known-good charger and outlet had
already been tested. Based on the evidence, the issue was identified as a hardware failure.
Resolution
Verified the reported troubleshooting with the Facilities lead. Built a replacement laptop using the
Computer Deployment tool with Manual Domain Enrollment. Installed required corporate
applications (VPN, Microsoft Teams, Mail, and standard software). Registered the laptop in Asset
Management, marked it Ready, checked it out to the onsite warehouse, obtained the receiving dock
address, created a same-day shipment with a prepaid return label, confirmed delivery, and
arranged the return of the failed laptop for repair or disposal.
Outcome
Warehouse operations resumed using the replacement laptop while the failed hardware entered the
repair/replacement process.
Skills Demonstrated
Hardware troubleshooting, business continuity, Windows deployment, manual domain enrollment,
software provisioning, asset lifecycle management, enterprise logistics, and professional IT
documentation
